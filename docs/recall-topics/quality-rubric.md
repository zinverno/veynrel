| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Self-contained question | Ambiguous referent or answer revealed | Some context missing | Clear subject and one testable idea |
| Source grounding | Invented/contradictory answer | Partly supported | Answer supported by the selected source |
| Conditions and distinctions | Important negation/caveat lost | Imprecise | Relevant limits retained |
| Coverage | Repeats the first definition | Some important ideas | Several important ideas represented |
| Non-repetition | Paraphrased duplicates | Mild overlap | Distinct recall/explanation/application value |

Any invented answer or reversal of a critical condition fails the sample regardless of its total score. A lower-than-requested count is acceptable when source support is insufficient. Schema/parser success is a separate technical result, not a pedagogical score. Record model quality as untested until actual outputs have been assessed.
